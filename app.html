Wayfarer

Travel guides & booking tools

A free travel guide for your phone: where to stay in popular cities, trip-prep tips and quick links to trusted booking sites, all in one place.

Get it on Google Play Or open the web version

What you get

  • Where-to-stay guides for 10 cities: Lisbon, Tokyo, Bangkok, Mexico City, Rome, Punta Cana, Cartagena, New York, Marrakech and Cape Town.
  • Trip-prep tips: packing light, staying connected, insurance and documents.
  • Quick booking: a numbered list that follows a real trip, from flights to hotels, tours, eSIM data and travel insurance.
  • Embassy directory to find your country's embassy abroad.
  • Your language: the app follows your phone's language automatically.

Free, no account needed

Wayfarer is free and does not ask you to sign up. Partner sites open on top of the app, and the X brings you back. Bookings and payments are made directly with each partner.

Some links are affiliate links: we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. That is how the app stays free.
