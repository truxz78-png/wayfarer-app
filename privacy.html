Privacy Policy for Wayfarer

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Wayfarer ("the App", "we", "us", or "our"), published by DriftforgeDev, an independent developer based in the Dominican Republic, is an affiliate travel discovery application that helps users find flights, hotels, tours, eSIM data plans, travel insurance, and travel guides through links to third-party travel platforms. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with your use of the App.

1. Information We Collect

Wayfarer does not require account creation or login, and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or payment details directly within the App.

The App does not use analytics or tracking tools, and we do not collect usage statistics.

To work, the App uses a few technical services. When the App loads, your device connects to them, and they may receive standard technical data such as your IP address and device or browser type, as any website does:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the App's content.
  • Google Fonts provides the App's fonts.
  • Google translation service translates the App's text into your phone's language when it is not English. Only the App's own text is sent for translation, never anything you type.

These services handle that data under their own privacy policies. We do not receive or store it.

The App saves small amounts of data only on your own device (local storage): translated texts, so they load faster next time, and your place on the page. This data stays on your phone, is not sent to us, and is deleted if you clear the App's storage.

2. Third-Party Affiliate Links

Wayfarer contains affiliate links to third-party travel service providers, including but not limited to Kayak, Aviasales, Booking.com, WeGoTrip, Airalo, and SafetyWing, some of them through affiliate networks such as Stay22 and Travelpayouts. When you tap on any of these links, you will be taken to the website or app of the third party, which operates under its own privacy policy and terms of service, entirely independent of Wayfarer.

We do not control and are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or actions of these third-party providers. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any third-party site before providing any personal or payment information.

3. Advertising Identifier

Wayfarer does not use the Advertising ID and does not display in-app advertisements.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any personal information with third parties, because we do not collect personal information through the App itself. Any data shared with a third-party travel provider occurs directly between you and that provider once you leave the App via an affiliate link.

5. Children's Privacy

Wayfarer is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data Security

Since Wayfarer does not collect or store personal user data on its own servers, there is no personal data at risk of being compromised through the App itself.

7. Terms of Service

Your use of the App is also governed by our Terms of Service.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
